S1 · SPACE
Your Abundance Corner
a dedicated place for your finances

A ritual without a space is just a habit that fades. Before you can change your relationship with money, you need a physical place where that relationship lives.

Set up your corner
✓
Choose a spot. Desk, shelf, or table corner.
Somewhere you can sit undisturbed for 10 minutes.
✓
Clear everything that does not belong.
This space is only for your abundance practice.
✓
Add one intentional element.
A candle, a plant, your Abundance Bowl. Something that signals calm.
✓
Sit in it for two minutes right now.
Notice how it feels. This is where your practice lives.

THE ABUNDANCE BOWL

A beautiful bowl placed in your Abundance Corner. Each day, you place something in it that represents what you are grateful for financially. A coin, a note, a small object. It becomes a physical record of your growing relationship with abundance.
