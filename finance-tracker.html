Why consumer debt is the most dangerous

Consumer debt (credit cards, personal loans, buy-now-pay-later) carries the highest interest rates, often 20-30% annually. A $5,000 credit card balance at 24% APR costs you $1,200 per year in interest alone. That money could be building your future instead of paying for your past.

The Quiet Abundance method locks your focus here first. No savings goals, no investments, until this weight is lifted.

Understanding your interest

Credit cards typically charge 20-30% APR. Personal loans 8-15%. Car loans 5-10%. The higher the rate, the more urgently that debt needs to go. We attack the highest-rate debt first (avalanche method) while making minimums on everything else.
