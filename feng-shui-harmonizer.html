Why Feng Shui matters
Feng Shui is not decoration. It is a 3,000-year-old system of spatial medicine. The five elements — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — are not metaphors; they describe how energy (chi) moves through a room and through you. When your space is dominated by one element, your nervous system responds. An all-white, all-metal room amplifies rigidity and contraction. A room drowning in dark tones pulls energy inward until it stagnates. Balance is not aesthetic — it is physiological. Your environment is either healing you or depleting you, every hour you spend in it.

Colour is elemental energy
Every colour carries an element. White and grey are Metal — precision but also coldness. Green is Wood — growth and vitality. Red and orange are Fire — passion and warmth. Beige and yellow are Earth — stability and nourishment. Blue and black are Water — depth and flow. A room that is all one colour family creates an elemental monoculture that starves you of what you need.
Materials present in your space
Select all materials you can see or touch in the room. Each material carries elemental energy that either supports or undermines your ritual practice.
The productive & destructive cycles
Elements interact in two cycles. The productive cycle: Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth (ash), Earth bears Metal, Metal collects Water (condensation), Water nourishes Wood. The destructive cycle: Wood parts Earth, Earth dams Water, Water extinguishes Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood. Your space must honour these relationships.

The Bagua map
The Bagua is an octagonal energy map that divides your home into eight sectors, each governing a life area. North rules career and life path (Water). South rules fame and reputation (Fire). East rules health and family (Wood). West rules creativity and children (Metal). The diagonal sectors add nuance: SE is wealth (Wood), SW is love (Earth), NE is knowledge (Earth), NW is helpful people (Metal). Your ritual space's direction determines which elemental energy it should amplify.
